# Kumano Kodo photo album

10 selected photographs from `熊野古道.zip` supplied by the owner, curated in narrative order.
All images are derived **without crops, added filters, or new watermarks** from supplied JPEGs.
`mg-9178-1.webp` retains 2560 × 3840 pixels of the uploaded ZIP source, and replaces the former 1365 × 2048 version in the website home and album.
Other lightbox WebPs use up to 3200 pixels on the longest edge. Thumbnails are separately optimized.
`cover.webp` is the album index thumbnail. Original JPEGs remain outside the published site; retain the uploaded ZIP in private storage.

Not selected in the public album: `_MG_9073-1-4.jpg`, `_MG_9111-1.jpg`, `_MG_9151-1.jpg`, `_MG_9298-1.jpg`.
These can be restored by converting from original JPEG and adding entries to `data/albums.js`.
